# Next steps — AI on Wikipedia

This is the human handoff memo for AI on Wikipedia, the shareable companion to the `ROADMAP.md` checklist. Read it to learn what was just built, what to do next, and who owns it, without opening a tracker or a board. The assignable, checkbox version of everything below lives in `ROADMAP.md`. This page lets a PI or stakeholder get oriented in a couple of minutes.

We scaffolded AI on Wikipedia at level **L2 (Reproduce)**, sized for a single-PI manuscript on a short clock: LOI due 2026-10-25, and the manuscript due 2027-01-15 for the JQD:DM Special Workflow Issue. The repository structure, documentation, a pinned Python environment, CI, and a Snakemake pipeline are in place. The front half of the pipeline works end to end: language ranking, category crawl, frame construction through Wikidata, relevance-audit sampling, dump download, and pageviews. The back half (revisions, panel, describe) is stubbed, with the target schema already written into `DATA-DICTIONARY.md`. The research design and the four LOI answers are drafted in `docs/design-memo.md` and `docs/loi-draft.md`.

The top next actions are tracked as an assignable checklist in `ROADMAP.md` under "This level's work". Treat that table as the live to-do list and check items off as their definition of done is met. Default ownership sits with **the PI (Brian C. Keegan)** unless a row names a role such as data engineer. Reassign rows there rather than tracking owners in your head, so there is one place that answers "who is doing what." In short: before the LOI, verify the language ranking, pin the seed category, build the frame, and put the real frame sizes into the LOI. After the LOI, implement revisions with editor pseudonymization, then panel and describe.

Two items are marked **blocking** in `ROADMAP.md`. The LOI deadline is fixed. Editor-identifier pseudonymization must be in place before any revision data is written. A conditional blocking item also gates any release of editor-level or coauthorship-network data on governance (access tiers, salt custody, remedy). Do not route around a blocking item quietly. Raising it here is how a stakeholder learns the project is paused for a good reason.

To climb to the next level, re-run the `data-project` skill in this directory and ask for the next rung (for example, "take this to L3" when coauthors join). It adds only the new level's artifacts, refreshes `ROADMAP.md`, and leaves what already works untouched.
